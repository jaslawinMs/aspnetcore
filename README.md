# ASP.NET Core AutoPerf evaluations

This branch contains the candidate inventory, reproducible benchmark projects,
and raw results for independently evaluated optimizations. Code proposals live
on separate branches and are submitted as draft PRs **inside this fork**, not
against dotnet/aspnetcore.

The [complete candidate inventory](findings.md) preserves all 358 remaining
findings and their original IDs. Eleven exact overlaps with existing work from
dotnet/aspnetcore#70219 were excluded. Pending findings are not confirmed defects
or measured regressions.

**Progress: 157/358 finalized**: 78 drafts, 26 no-gain, 28 rejected and 25 duplicates;
201 findings remain unfinished.

**Start here:** [Classified findings report and linked artifact map](REPORT.md)
connects measured proposals to their code PRs, tracking issues, benchmark projects,
full results and local test evidence, with trade-offs and non-obvious equivalence explained.

Each item receives at most three measured before/after comparisons. A clear
large win can stop early; non-improving items are marked `no-gain`, not published
as speculative optimization PRs. Incompatible replacements are marked
`rejected` even when individual benchmarks improve. Correctness, actual owning-component behavior,
small-workload controls, allocations, and measurement limitations matter as
well as the largest speedup.

**Execution-policy clarification:** Earlier stock BDN 0.13.0 executors attempt to raise their own
process/thread priorities. The success of those attempts and actual historical settings were
not captured, so earlier blanket "no power/priority/affinity changes" notes are not verified
normal-priority timing evidence. F113 independently uses a settings-preserving emitted
executor with before/setup/after observations; earlier measurements are not rerun or
relabeled. See the [execution policy and historical clarification](F113/README.md#shared-machine-execution-policy-and-historical-clarification).

**Harness-identity clarification:** F004 used separately built, byte-different harness DLL/PDBs
despite identical harness source. Its conservative no-gain disposition is unchanged, but
its three paired observations must not be interpreted as exact-harness owner isolation.
The [additive identity observation](F004-harness-identity-clarification.json) records the
actual differences; the immutable F004 capsule is unchanged and no benchmark was rerun.

**F032 file-count clarification:** Its measured 186-file consumer has two changed HTTP.sys
owner files and 184 unchanged files **including** the harness DLL/PDB, leaving 182 other
unchanged files. Some archived prose incorrectly says "184 other files" in addition to
the harness pair. The [additive count clarification](F032-consumer-count-clarification.json)
does not change the immutable capsule, measurements, no-gain outcome, or campaign progress.
