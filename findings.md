Pending findings are not confirmed defects, measured regressions, or approved fixes.

Each item receives at most three measured before/after comparisons. Only a justified, correctness-checked optimization receives a fork-only draft PR; resolved, duplicate, incompatible and no-gain items are recorded rather than manufactured into changes.
Measurements are local microbenchmarks on a shared Windows box, not production throughput claims.
